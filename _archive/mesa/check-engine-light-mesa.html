Steady vs. flashing

What your check engine light is telling you

Steady light: something needs attention soon — drive gently and get it scanned before a small problem becomes an expensive one. Flashing light: pull over safely and stop driving. That usually means an active misfire that can wreck your catalytic converter in minutes. Either way, you don't need to tow it anywhere — we bring the scanner to you.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 20, 2026

LetsWrench Mobile Mechanic is committed to making our website accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Accessibility Goal

We aim to follow Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards where reasonably possible for this website.

Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive controls.
  • Visible focus indicators on links, buttons, and form fields.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Semantic page structure using headings and landmarks.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review the site regularly and make updates to improve usability for all visitors.

Need Help?

If you have difficulty using this website or need information in an alternate format, contact us and we will do our best to assist promptly.
